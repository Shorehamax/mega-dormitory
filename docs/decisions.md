# Personality and social choices — M01.3

Residents have persistent generosity, conscience, courage and sociability, alongside their existing aggression, interests, needs and directional relationships. New worlds and incoming residents derive traits from seed and identity through separate hashes. This does not consume the world RNG or rewrite generated names, opening wealth or jobs. Older native saves and browser imports default the new traits to neutral values and gain no invented decision history.

## Connected behavior

- **Help and refusal:** spare-meal requests require real local contact, two usable owned meals, recipient need, the donor's own reserve, and both participants' consent. Generosity can motivate helping a stranger. A selfish stranger can refuse, while friendship earned through actual encounters can change a later decision. Recognized violence/theft and accumulated grievances matter. Sleeping, absent, fighting or restrained actors cannot make the handover. Existing aid cooldowns prevent repeated gifts from farming rewards. A household helper considers willingness before starting a real walking errand and checks again at arrival.
- **Cooperation:** reserved residents may decline unknown contacts. Recognized recent harm or a strong grievance can prevent supplying a requested report, agreeing to a seller meeting, or an NPC information exchange. Friendship can overcome reserve. Directly commanding your controlled actor to send a known report remains a deliberate player action. Refusal does not leak an address, invent a report, transfer ownership or create eyewitness evidence.
- **Theft:** queued automatic residents still need a rare opportunity and a genuinely reachable, unreserved, unworn owned item. Conscience, desperation, poverty, aggression and directional relationships influence the attempt. A visible nearby warden deters it; a hidden guard on another floor does not. The actor still calls the real theft action, with its success/failure, stolen ownership, witnesses, retaliation and criminal consequences. Successful theft withdraws an existing sale listing. Manual player control never initiates an automatic theft attempt.
- **Reporting:** autonomous witnesses consider conscience, courage, known loyalty/grievance, victim status, severity and retaliation from a visible identified offender. An unknown culprit's identity, strength and relationship are not consulted. A timid witness can defer and report when the danger actually leaves. Refusal by one witness cannot suppress another's report. Interview and callback requests respect an automatic witness's decision; willingness cannot replace the existing evidence, contact and reporting requirements. A warden who actually observed the incident reports as a professional duty. Explicit reports commanded by the player remain voluntary actions.
- **Surrender:** current health, energy, observed relative strength, courage, aggression, recognized prior violence and lawful warden pressure affect yielding. A loaded gun affects this assessment when the visible opponent draws it in combat, not merely because it exists in concealed inventory. Critical injury, exhaustion or overwhelming strength makes even brave residents prefer surrender. Automatic fighters reassess before retaliating. Real surrender still changes combat state, releases opponents and permits physical apprehension; no automatic remote detention or teleportation is added.

These are bounded decisions that use the existing action systems. Willingness is not proof of a completed action. An unavailable route, expired contact, changed resources or lack of evidence can still prevent success. No policy issues rewards, money, new food, items, warrants or damage by itself. Existing staffing, job duties, ownership, accounting, movement and legal responsibility remain authoritative.

## Player presentation and persistence

In **My life**, expand **Personality & decisions**. It opens at the section and shows the four traits and the four latest decisions with their time, known contact, willingness/refusal and localized reason. It is collapsed by default. The resident retains eight recent decisions; identical repeated polling within five game minutes does not spam the history. These notes are private to the inspected/controlled actor, not globally shared observations. The [M01.4 history book](social-history.md) exposes both pages of the saved decisions and the full retained encounter history.

Traits, reasons, actual historical target IDs and timestamps round-trip with saves. Death and departure retain past identities and decisions. Validation checks trait ranges/finite values, known reason IDs, subject types, history bounds and chronological clocks. Unknown events do not become known through a decision assessment.

## Verification

`personality_and_social_choices` has 41 focused cases, covering opposite choices in equal circumstances, a decision changed by real friendship, recipient refusal, hidden-crime privacy, resources and reach, actual queued theft, witness loyalty/fear/duty, independent witnesses, interviews/callbacks, drawn firearms, surrender, immigration, old saves and malformed records.

`--playtest-decisions` runs an isolated real scenario: a selfish resident refuses a local request; a willing housemate chooses a household errand, walks continuously and hands over an actual spare meal with consent. It renders both residents' reasons, saves/reloads the real transfer and checks conserved money and item ownership. `docs/evidence/decisions-verification.json` records the completed checks.
