# Lockers and household pantries

Open **My life → Storage & household pantry**, or interact with a locker facility nearby. Walk to its counter to rent a compartment. A private lease protects personal belongings; a household lease can also hold explicitly shared food, medicine and clothes. Initial facilities have eight compartments, each with twelve real shelf positions. Owners can commission more facilities through ordinary funded construction.

Rent is paid to the current operator. Daily renewals use the holder's own account. Missed rent becomes debt; four unpaid days make the lease collection-only. The owner does not destroy or confiscate the contents. Empty released compartments become available again; unpaid debt remains collectible. An active lease blocks renovation/demolition. Selling the facility retains existing leases and belongings.

Reach your compartment and open its sliding door to deposit or retrieve. Each deposit keeps its original owner. **Store privately** grants no household permission. **Share with household** permits current housemates to take that specific supply; title transfers on actual collection. Devices, valuables, equipment and stolen goods cannot become a shared donation. You cannot use a stored phone/laptop remotely. Taking items requires an open door and physical reach.

Hungry housemates can walk to an available pantry before purchasing another meal. Donors keep a personal carried meal before automatically contributing surplus. A resident who leaves the household loses access to other donors' supplies but can collect their own belongings. Leaving the dormitory requires collecting private stored property first. Automatic emigrants physically visit their compartments before heading to the lobby. If a custodian dies or leaves, remaining owners retain collection access; nobody else's goods disappear.

An unlocked locker can be stolen from. Actual ownership/provenance remains tracked, and witnesses or later discovery produce the normal theft process. An absent owner does not magically identify a thief. A warden must physically open an authorized accessible compartment before seizing evidence; the ordinary jail-desk recovery process still applies.

Leases, debt, contents, sharing permissions and exact shelf positions save with the world. Older saves retain their layout; build a locker facility through construction rather than expecting an old floor to change automatically. Stored-property relations are validated during loading.
