# Communal washing

In new modern-layout worlds, washbasins start in the lobby and beside laundries every twenty floors. Existing saves keep their layouts; an owner can commission a washbasin through Management, fund the plot and construction, and have a builder collect and deliver materials before opening it. The person funding construction owns it; a facility operator receives service payments under the existing ownership/lease rules.

Walk to a washbasin through Services, use E nearby, or choose **My life → Walk to a washbasin** when carrying dirty dishes. My life shows cleanliness, queue position or remaining washing time, and a cancellation control. The interface supports English, French and Chinese. Close observer views remain framed beside the menu instead of rotating away when it opens.

A visit reserves one water-and-soap supply kit and a real station. Up to four of the dirtiest eligible owned tableware/cooker items are placed at the facility and become unavailable for use, trade or theft during service. Computers, phones, fans, clothes, broken items and belongings owned by someone else are excluded. Clothes still use the laundry. Cooker washing represents cleaning its removable food-contact parts; it is not a component-disassembly simulation.

Default visits take three game minutes and cost 100 cents. Charges and cleanliness restoration occur only on completion. Items keep their durability: washing is not repair. Cancellation, unaffordable completion or death releases unwashed items and the reserved kit. A closed or broken facility pauses the existing service; reservations and remaining work survive save/reload. A hand-only visit improves hygiene up to 80%, without replacing a full shower.

Owners may change prices, access and opening hours. Basins need no on-site worker and initially open around the clock, but require supplies and maintenance. Restocking uses the existing funded import crates and physical courier jobs; discarded kit packaging enters facility waste and the cleaner/depot loop. Water and soap are represented by one stock bundle rather than separate plumbing and fluid simulation.

Tableware and cooker items below 25% cleanliness cannot start another household activity. Residents with tools below 40% seek an affordable, stocked basin during free time, after urgent needs. People may still eat an available food pack without equipment. New washing rules do not cancel a household activity already running in an older save.

Basin meshes have walking collisions. Banks support up to sixteen stations in two spaced rows, with standing and queue space kept clear. The geometry and animation are procedural and use the existing GPU/CPU character paths; contact IK and detailed component meshes remain future work.
