## Finite scope rules

- All 38 requirements and 14 parity rows belong to existing M00–M32; no extra milestone was introduced.
- Family scope is the retained browser lifecycle plus causal dependent care, not an unlimited family-law simulator.
- Evidence/search, structural integrity and smoke are bounded gameplay models; scientific fluid simulation, an entire court system and deformable car bodies are not additional release blockers.
- Preserve historical browser counters as labelled pre-conversion history; never replay their transactions into native balances.
- Platform claims require real validation. Linux is primary; Windows remains a recorded plan gate, not verified support.
