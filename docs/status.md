# Native implementation status

This is a development build, not a declaration that the complete accepted design has shipped. The reference-image fidelity and production polish have not been achieved. The former island/city and Creative mode are superseded by the dormitory-focused requirements in `requirements.md`.

## Latest browser conversion and household activities

- Load opens a localized browser-export conversion form, with path entry, Survival/Manager/Cheat selection, background loading and a persistent conversion report. Successful conversion opens a new session without autosaving. Source exports are protected against saving to the same or an equivalent filesystem path. Existing native saves remain compatible.
- Version-1/2 gzip or plain JSON imports retain IDs, corresponding names, age/sex, needs, infection/immunity, wallets, institutional/business funds, property/tenancy, all six old personal-item kinds, queue order and prepaid service progress. Native staffing/geometry/schedules replace old event routing; completed historical services are not paid again. Imported prepaid cancellations retain their original payment. Departed cash remains outside the building economy. New civic buildings have no invented funds or stock. See `importing.md` for exact policies.
- Browser-only family, pregnancy, birth, history, experimental parameters and cumulative accounts remain in a bounded native archive, rather than silently disappearing. Their behavior is **not yet ported**. Historical dead residents do not create fresh bodies. Full browser parity remains required.
- Fans, tableware and rice cookers now have native timed activities and DORMNET prices with real escrow/pickup/delivery. Use requires ownership, working equipment, access to the resident’s bed and actual food when needed. Completion consumes food once, creates waste, applies bounded benefits and wears/dirties equipment. NPCs can choose their own possessions. Interruptions, movement, robbery, combat, surrender and death cancel work; unfinished ingredients remain. Active tools/food cannot also be sold or consumed. Progress saves/loads. Effects remain procedural; electrical/thermal appliance systems and a full cooking minigame are unfinished.
- Imported dirty coats map to cleanliness without falsely becoming broken. Device condition now gates network access. Staffing setup excludes historical dead/departed residents; children are not assigned initial staff shifts. Native world validation accepts legitimate imported/custom housing populations beyond the generator’s template-bed limit, while new generation still enforces it.

The protected-import address/undefined-behavior/leak run passes all five suites (270.15 seconds total). The three catalogues contain **708 matching keys**. - An actual 600-person browser export converted and continued for one native hour with no blocked-task trace errors. ## Latest named saves and multilingual input

- Save/Load now opens an eight-slot browser from world creation, Settings and game over. Names, modes, game times and populations are visible without decompressing the resident arrays. The simulation pauses while browsing; cancellation restores its previous pause state. Replacement requires two clicks. Loading warns that unsaved progress will be replaced.
- Bounded sidecars are tied to the native file size and Zstandard checksum tag. Missing, corrupt, oversized or stale metadata falls back to an unnamed preview and never becomes authoritative state. Backup-only slots are listed. Both automatic corruption recovery and explicit previous-backup loading are supported. A recovered primary is repaired without overwriting its good backup with the old corrupt file.
- Saving nearby walking residents now uses their actual fitness-dependent walking speed when rebuilding the remaining saved route, instead of the old 1.6 m/s constant.
- Text glyphs are appended on demand to a bounded 4096-pixel atlas, preserving existing UVs. Chinese save names and French accents can use supported font characters beyond the fixed menu catalogue. The Vulkan texture updates only when new glyphs are added. Long text inputs scroll to keep their caret visible. Platform IME integration remains unfinished.
- Release verification: **189 simulation checks, asset imports and the multilingual UI suite pass** (6.95 seconds total). Address/undefined-behavior/leak checking passed: simulation 254.23 seconds, assets 10.26 seconds, UI 5.66 seconds. French RTX and Chinese software-Vulkan menus were captured at 1000 × 720 with validation enabled and no validation errors. All **665 locale keys** match across English, French and Chinese.

## Latest fire, evacuation and recovery systems

- New worlds have rare seeded facility fires and an accident-frequency choice. Legacy saves without this setting load with new accidents disabled; their existing layouts are preserved. Fire stations can be built through funded construction, and extinguishers can be ordered through DORMNET and physically delivered.
- Fires consume stock, damage facility condition, expose residents to heat/smoke and close affected services. Smoke affects a local radius and up to three floors above the source. This is a localized hazard model, not a fluid simulation or structural-collapse model. Damaged businesses still need the normal parts/materials/repair workflow.
- NPCs leave queues and walk to reachable safe points. Civilians avoid hazardous route cells; equipped responders can approach the source. Unsafe home/work destinations temporarily suspend the corresponding routine rather than repeatedly planning failed trips. Manual players receive a building alarm and an evacuation action.
- A fire station is an owned, stock-holding facility with real firefighter shifts. Initial departments belong to the dormitory and pay standby wages from its treasury. Private ownership, hiring, opening hours and supplied equipment use the existing business rules. Nearby smoke does not lock the self-service equipment rack; a fire in the station itself closes it.
- A response contract escrows 18,000 cents from the requesting business, dormitory owner or a voluntary sponsor. The responder collects actual equipment at their station and travels to the scene. Money is paid only for on-site suppression, split between labor and the provider, with a hard contract cap and refunds of unused funds after smoke clears. No equipment or wages are credited for an unfunded dispatch or failed route.
- Players and NPCs can use a charged extinguisher within seven metres and a clear line of sight. Pressure and energy decline during work. Interruptions, depleted equipment, changed shifts, changed tasks and death release the call. Casualties enter the existing remains/medical/crime cleanup pipeline. Station supplies require actual restocking and delivery.
- Localized Fire & rescue controls sit under My life; an active alarm opens them directly. Firefighter work, public equipment purchases, response sponsorship, physical approaches, spray/stop controls and Cheat-mode test fires are playable. Fire glow and bounded smoke shading run in the Vulkan fragment shader, with animated flames and nozzle particles. This pass still uses procedural visual effects and poses.
- The end-of-run overlay now blocks play controls and offers slot selection, reload (including an available backup) and the main menu. Loading a world clears stale menu/minigame selections and animation history. Emergency action errors are localized and long notifications wrap within the window.
- **183 simulation checks and the asset suite pass.** The final address/undefined-behavior/leak run passed the simulation in 250.91 seconds and assets in 10.25 seconds. These include collection/payment, real delivery, travel, suppression, evacuation, shift changes, casualties, refunds, persistence and malformed-state checks.
- An integrated 1,000-resident run extinguished a fire with a real dispatch and five evacuation routes, paid 10,296 cents for actual labor/service, and saved/reloaded/continued successfully; the trace recorded zero blocked tasks. A 100,000-resident / 2,000-floor run also extinguished its fire, paid 5,789 cents for response work, retained all 100,000 residents, and saved/reloaded/continued. Its trace also reported zero blocked tasks. The bounded world journal is not an authoritative lifetime event counter.
- An older 100,000-resident save loaded, advanced one hour and saved successfully (3.78 seconds load, 29.84 seconds run/save on this PC). English/French RTX and Chinese lavapipe captures, including 1000 × 720 menus, passed Vulkan validation. The three catalogues contain **652 matching keys**.

## Latest company and workplace systems

- Work → Companies & teams creates a company using personal capital. Company money, private money, client escrow, landlord revenue and refundable workspace deposits remain distinct. Owners can invest and take dividends only after reserving accrued wages, a payroll shift and three days of rent.
- Offices can be bought, contributed by their personal owner, or leased from a posted offer. Leasing retains the landlord's property title while moving current employees, operating revenue, supply/repair costs and computer access to the tenant company. Transfers settle old payroll and refund unfinished client commissions, including legacy video jobs.
- Leases charge a day upfront plus a two-day deposit. Rent accrues daily; catch-up payments clear the oldest debt. Three unpaid days end the lease. Deposits cover only actual arrears and the rest returns to the company; uncovered debt and unpaid wage claims persist. Employees are released and unfinished work refunded. Active leases prevent property sales and construction from bypassing the tenant's operating rights.
- Owners and team leaders assign actual programming, video or administration briefs. New assignments wait for the current funded commission to finish. Company revenue and individual quality deliveries are recorded. Leaders can manage work and recruitment but cannot withdraw capital or dispose of property.
- AI companies recruit willing local unemployed residents into funded shift vacancies, rotate briefs and promote workers after quality deliveries. They do not teleport workers to desks. This is a basic staffing policy, not a complete autonomous investment strategy.
- Owners can commission an office from their company treasury. It remains closed until the existing material-delivery and construction-worker workflow finishes; the company paying for it owns the facility.
- Video editing now uses the same attended office-contract workflow as programming and administration. Three briefs ask for documentary, news or campaign structure. The UI retains unfinished older video commissions; operator changes refund them. The editor still uses labeled clip ordering, not playable video footage, trimming or audio editing.
- English, French and Chinese company menus include finance, spaces, staff, assignments and nearby property offers. Old saves without company data incorporate independent office business accounts; personal wallets and dormitory funds are never silently converted to company treasuries.

## Latest movement, rendering and interface changes

- The Survival HUD is now a minimal overlay. Tab/Esc opens an animated drawer containing the original detailed Life, Facilities, Work, Housing and Management panels, plus Journal and Settings. Health, energy, hunger and thirst remain visible. Settings holds language, graphics range, simulation time and save controls. Minimum-window French and Chinese layouts were captured; the save bar no longer overlaps the survival meters.
- Walking/running bases are 3.6/7.2 m/s; injury and fatigue affect movement. Saved fitness progressively adds up to 25% speed. Real walking/running/climbing and completed gym/pool sessions train strength/fitness with energy and nutritional costs. The ladder speed is 3 m/s with continuous landings.
- Path search falls back from a coarse district grid to quarter-metre clearance and a wider detour. Failed planning preserves the existing route. Full waiting areas trigger another service choice instead of task-interruption spam. Counter arrivals tolerate personal space, steering handles the final metre, and automatic customers physically walk away after service. Residents can smoothly leave pre-existing bed/body overlaps, but cannot deepen them. Invisible upper floors no longer leave floating residents in cutaway view. Service stock/queue reservations commit after routing succeeds.
- Concrete lighting uses a stable floor boundary; shadow anchoring no longer shifts when only the view rotates. Material mip levels, footprint-aware tile guards and optional anisotropic filtering reduce texture bleeding and distant shimmer. The main render pass uses 4× MSAA when supported (2× fallback); software Vulkan stays single-sample.
- Distant structures use a cached instance buffer and a single instanced draw call. The GPU transforms repeated slabs, bed proxies, rails, ladders and facility proxies. The default range is 96 floors; settings offer 32/96/192, with software rendering capped at 24. Detailed nearby geometry is retained. The observer has a cutaway toggle to hide or reveal floors above the selected one; this is not an all-2,000-floors detailed renderer. CPU simulation and CPU skeletal skinning are unchanged.
- 147 simulation checks pass, including narrow-aisle clearance, route rollback, fitness persistence, paid gym use and physical counter congestion without overlap. The 600-resident one-day trace reports no task-interruption errors (the prior build produced 89 full-waiting-area interruptions in two hours). A 100,000-resident / 2,000-floor two-hour trace also reports none, with all 100,000 living and 18,965 queued. The final 147-check simulation suite also passes address/undefined-behavior/leak sanitizers in 227.29 seconds; the unchanged asset suite passed the same checks in 10.65 seconds. These are bounded regression checks, not a long-term balance claim.
- RTX and lavapipe captures pass Vulkan validation with the new storage-buffer instances. The 96-floor atrium capture submits 42,491 instances; the bounded software capture submits 8,986. Older performance figures below describe the earlier build.

## Work still required for the accepted finished game

- Imported animation clips/retargeting, modular clothing, contact IK and expressive combat/interaction animation beyond the implemented weighted bodies and blended procedural poses, high-quality character/environment art, sound and comprehensive accessibility/input QA.
- Broader geometry LOD/streaming and GPU skinning/simulation work; streamed geometry rather than regenerating large CPU meshes; more complete lighting/contact shadows and materials.
- Comprehensive moving-platform/furniture/vehicle contacts, ramps/freight-lift vehicle transport, energy/fuel, damage, towing and autonomous traffic.
- General interior-floor removal and arbitrary layout editing, construction teams/foremen and detailed negotiation/eviction workflows.
- Company shareholding, mergers, succession/estates, autonomous workspace expansion and richer negotiated contracts; deeper video editing, broader administrative workflows, fuller schooling and job disciplines.
- Broader autonomous social/trading behavior, estates and personal inheritance, witness/investigation/stolen-property recovery, restraints/escapes and richer legal consequences.
- Broader fire spread, damaged personal property, structural failure and further disaster/response types beyond infection and the implemented localized fires; sustained economy/scarcity/AI investment balancing across population, dimensions and time settings.
- Full Cheat world-rule editing, complete browser feature parity beyond the implemented one-way conversion, save-format migration guarantees and Windows packaging.

Passing automated tests means the tested rules hold. It does not mean the complete game or its visual quality is finished.
