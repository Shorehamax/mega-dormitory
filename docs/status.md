# Native implementation status

This is a development build, not a declaration that the complete accepted design has shipped. The reference-image fidelity and production polish have not been achieved. The former island/city and Creative mode are superseded by the dormitory-focused requirements in `requirements.md`.

## Latest company and workplace systems

- Work → Companies & teams creates a company using personal capital. Company money, private money, client escrow, landlord revenue and refundable workspace deposits remain distinct. Owners can invest and take dividends only after reserving accrued wages, a payroll shift and three days of rent.
- Offices can be bought, contributed by their personal owner, or leased from a posted offer. Leasing retains the landlord's property title while moving current employees, operating revenue, supply/repair costs and computer access to the tenant company. Transfers settle old payroll and refund unfinished client commissions, including legacy video jobs.
- Leases charge a day upfront plus a two-day deposit. Rent accrues daily; catch-up payments clear the oldest debt. Three unpaid days end the lease. Deposits cover only actual arrears and the rest returns to the company; uncovered debt and unpaid wage claims persist. Employees are released and unfinished work refunded. Active leases prevent property sales and construction from bypassing the tenant's operating rights.
- Owners and team leaders assign actual programming, video or administration briefs. New assignments wait for the current funded commission to finish. Company revenue and individual quality deliveries are recorded. Leaders can manage work and recruitment but cannot withdraw capital or dispose of property.
- AI companies recruit willing local unemployed residents into funded shift vacancies, rotate briefs and promote workers after quality deliveries. They do not teleport workers to desks. This is a basic staffing policy, not a complete autonomous investment strategy.
- Owners can commission an office from their company treasury. It remains closed until the existing material-delivery and construction-worker workflow finishes; the company paying for it owns the facility.
- Video editing now uses the same attended office-contract workflow as programming and administration. Three briefs ask for documentary, news or campaign structure. The UI retains unfinished older video commissions; operator changes refund them. The editor still uses labeled clip ordering, not playable video footage, trimming or audio editing.
- English, French and Chinese company menus include finance, spaces, staff, assignments and nearby property offers. Old saves without company data incorporate independent office business accounts; personal wallets and dormitory funds are never silently converted to company treasuries.

## Latest movement, rendering and interface changes

- The Survival HUD is now a minimal overlay. Tab/Esc opens an animated drawer containing the original detailed Life, Facilities, Work, Housing and Management panels, plus Journal and Settings. Health, energy, hunger and thirst remain visible. Settings holds language, graphics range, simulation time and save controls. Minimum-window French and Chinese layouts were captured; the save bar no longer overlaps the survival meters.
- Walking/running bases are 3.6/7.2 m/s; injury and fatigue affect movement. Saved fitness progressively adds up to 25% speed. Real walking/running/climbing and completed gym/pool sessions train strength/fitness with energy and nutritional costs. The ladder speed is 3 m/s with continuous landings.
- Path search falls back from a coarse district grid to quarter-metre clearance and a wider detour. Failed planning preserves the existing route. Full waiting areas trigger another service choice instead of task-interruption spam. Counter arrivals tolerate personal space, steering handles the final metre, and automatic customers physically walk away after service. Residents can smoothly leave pre-existing bed/body overlaps, but cannot deepen them. Invisible upper floors no longer leave floating residents in cutaway view. Service stock/queue reservations commit after routing succeeds.
- Concrete lighting uses a stable floor boundary; shadow anchoring no longer shifts when only the view rotates. Material mip levels, footprint-aware tile guards and optional anisotropic filtering reduce texture bleeding and distant shimmer. The main render pass uses 4× MSAA when supported (2× fallback); software Vulkan stays single-sample.
- Distant structures use a cached instance buffer and a single instanced draw call. The GPU transforms repeated slabs, bed proxies, rails, ladders and facility proxies. The default range is 96 floors; settings offer 32/96/192, with software rendering capped at 24. Detailed nearby geometry is retained. The observer has a cutaway toggle to hide or reveal floors above the selected one; this is not an all-2,000-floors detailed renderer. CPU simulation and CPU skeletal skinning are unchanged.
- 147 simulation checks pass, including narrow-aisle clearance, route rollback, fitness persistence, paid gym use and physical counter congestion without overlap. The 600-resident one-day trace reports no task-interruption errors (the prior build produced 89 full-waiting-area interruptions in two hours). A 100,000-resident / 2,000-floor two-hour trace also reports none, with all 100,000 living and 18,965 queued. The final 147-check simulation suite also passes address/undefined-behavior/leak sanitizers in 227.29 seconds; the unchanged asset suite passed the same checks in 10.65 seconds. These are bounded regression checks, not a long-term balance claim.
- RTX and lavapipe captures pass Vulkan validation with the new storage-buffer instances. The 96-floor atrium capture submits 42,491 instances; the bounded software capture submits 8,986. Older performance figures below describe the earlier build.

## Verification

Development PC: Ryzen 7 7800X3D, 32 GB RAM, RTX 5070, Linux/GCC 15.2. Results below are machine-specific and are not performance promises.

- The company update adds financial/ownership, lease/default, payroll, leadership, physical attendance, construction funding, computer permissions, legacy commission and persistence checks. Current release: **165 simulation checks plus the asset-import suite**, also passing in the installed build (7.12 seconds total). The final AddressSanitizer/UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer/LeakSanitizer run passed: simulation 234.26 seconds, asset imports 10.27 seconds.
- A 10,000-resident / 200-floor company run completed one day, saved, loaded, and advanced one more hour: 10,004 living, 11 companies, 67 completed jobs. The player-founded tenant earned 96,000 cents of work revenue, held 258,736 cents after expenses and had no rent arrears. This is a bounded workflow check, not proof of long-term balance.
- An older 100,000-resident / 2,000-floor save loaded, advanced an hour and saved with the company-capable format. All 100,000 residents remained alive; load took 3.33 seconds and simulation/save took 29.47 seconds on this PC.
- English company and video-workstation captures ran on RTX Vulkan; French company-team controls were checked at 1000 × 720. Chinese minimum-window controls ran on lavapipe CPU Vulkan. No validation errors were reported in these bounded captures.

- Earlier baseline: **139 simulation checks and the asset-import suite passed.** The address/undefined-behavior/leak run passed in 69.02 seconds. After the final skeletal LOD changes, the asset suite passed these checks again in 10.54 seconds. The simulation suite covers economic conservation, ownership/compensation, schedules/queues, station reuse, actual deliveries/materials, local repair sourcing, manager replacement, medical interruption/death, lifts, crime/escorts, housing changes, social locality, cheat bounds and save/recovery.
- Asset tests cover bundled models, material references/normals, binary GLB, OBJ transforms/negative indices and rejected path traversal. `tools/verify_assets.py` checks 59 downloaded Poly Haven/Kenney/Quaternius files against recorded hashes.
- RTX Vulkan and Mesa lavapipe were exercised with the new shadow pass and `VK_LAYER_KHRONOS_validation`; no validation errors were reported in the bounded three-frame captures. This is not exhaustive driver or interactive gameplay QA.
- GPU pressure computation: 100,000 entries, maximum absolute difference `5.96046e-08` versus the CPU formula. This pass is a verified rendering/compute foundation, not the authoritative simulation scheduler.
- English, French and Chinese native pages have been rendered. Their catalogue key sets are checked for equality. The live administration workflow was captured in Chinese, and the submitted result in French on software Vulkan. Platform IME integration remains unfinished.
- A pre-relief-staff full-size three-day run generated in 0.77 s and simulated/saved in 571.12 s: 95,854 living residents, 10,981 queued, happiness 0.381. Earlier runs exposed excessive long-distance errands, staff meal deadlocks, import travel, duplicated wear and single-manager supply failure. Those are being addressed; a three-day result does not establish a balanced long-term economy.

- The latest seven-day 10,000-resident / 200-floor run ended with 8,908 living and 895 queued, happiness 0.518; generation 0.04 s and run/save 83.50 s. This includes relief staffing, packed-meal choices, replacement business managers and corrected wear. Mortality remains significant and further balancing is required.
- The three localization catalogues contain 603 matching keys. Some diagnostic errors remain English.

- Character asset checks reproduce both source bind poses, exercise all ten activity poses, check mattress-height sleeping bounds, and independently verify a two-joint deformation and blended weights. Nearby characters have a capped detail budget; skinning is CPU-side. Seated/sleeping first-person camera heights now ease to their corresponding pose.
- An older native 100,000-resident save was loaded, advanced by one game hour and saved again successfully. This is a compatibility check, not a general migration guarantee.

- A 120-frame animated crowd capture under Vulkan validation improved from 10.56 s / 622,312 KiB peak RSS to 7.34 s / 489,908 KiB with skeletal LODs. These include startup and validation overhead and compare different character detail budgets; they are not a gameplay FPS measurement. The male/female derived LODs contain 3,085 / 2,760 triangles versus 14,318 / 15,060 source triangles.

## Work still required for the accepted finished game

- Imported animation clips/retargeting, modular clothing, contact IK and expressive combat/interaction animation beyond the implemented weighted bodies and blended procedural poses, high-quality character/environment art, sound and comprehensive accessibility/input QA.
- Broader geometry LOD/streaming and GPU skinning/simulation work; streamed geometry rather than regenerating large CPU meshes; more complete lighting/contact shadows and materials.
- Comprehensive moving-platform/furniture/vehicle contacts, ramps/freight-lift vehicle transport, energy/fuel, damage, towing and autonomous traffic.
- General interior-floor removal and arbitrary layout editing, construction teams/foremen and detailed negotiation/eviction workflows.
- Company shareholding, mergers, succession/estates, autonomous workspace expansion and richer negotiated contracts; deeper video editing, broader administrative workflows, fuller schooling and job disciplines.
- Broader autonomous social/trading behavior, estates and personal inheritance, witness/investigation/stolen-property recovery, restraints/escapes and richer legal consequences.
- More disaster types and response systems beyond infection, and sustained economy/scarcity/AI investment balancing across population, dimensions and time settings.
- Full Cheat world-rule editing, all browser feature parity/import, save-format migration guarantees and Windows packaging.

Passing automated tests means the tested rules hold. It does not mean the complete game or its visual quality is finished.
