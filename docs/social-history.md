# Private relationship and communication history — M01.4

Open **My life → Relationships & history**. The book identifies its viewer by stable resident number and localized name. Contacts, decisions and reports are separate views. Search accepts a name or resident number, with an optional `#`; English case, French accented letters/ligatures and Chinese text are handled. Filter by your own friendship/rivalry status. Opening another known contact does not possess them or expose their statistics or live position.

## What a resident can see

Known contacts come from their actual conversations, known encounter memories, historical directional opinions, household membership/invitations, delivered information, their own sent messages and received messages delivered through usable device access. An identified suspect named in a received report is known; an anonymous report does not reveal the incident's hidden offender. Original sources and messengers in delivered archived attachments remain known after an active report leaves the short notes cache.

Pending inbound messages expose neither sender nor attachment metadata to an offline recipient. A thief's private memory of an unnoticed theft does not create a named contact, grievance or history entry for its sleeping victim. The book does not consult global crime facts to fill gaps. It does not show a contact's current health, activity, possessions, position or whether an unobserved death has occurred. Displaying a historical contact is not proof that they can currently be reached.

Each contact opens all sixteen retained, known encounters, four per page, newest first. Entries show time, actual actor, event and the viewer's applied opinion change. Only the viewer's opinion is displayed. Opinions can survive after their bounded encounter display trims old entries; the book explains when no detailed encounters remain. Contact lists use four rows per page and name/ID filters. The decision view provides both pages of the resident's eight retained private decisions and reasons. Reports link to the existing source/time/place cards and physical sharing/visiting actions.

Changing the inspected resident clears the previous viewer's contact selection and cached data. Switching language changes labels and names, not resident IDs, opinions, message ownership, events or sources. Death and departure keep historical identities. Browsing alone changes no memory, relationship, RNG, money, inventory, observation or testimony.

## Conversations and report recall

**Messages** on a contact opens that resident's own conversation on a usable device, with the recipient prefilled. **Network → Messages** now pages through the full retained archive instead of stopping at eight messages. Filter a correspondent or return to all conversations. All text and structured attachments wrap and scroll; older pages remain accessible. Opening the device still requires real access, including paid public-terminal arrival, capacity, payment, proximity, hours, condition and session expiry. Sleeping residents cannot send or read messages. An unavailable recipient fails without appending a fictional delivery.

A delivered structured attachment can be **remembered** after it leaves the 24-report active cache. Recall preserves the original source, messenger, observation time, receipt time, reported place and freshness. It does not query the subject's current state, generate a new observation or replace a newer observation from the same source. Duplicate recall has no reward or duplicate. Another resident's inbox, undelivered copies, sent copies and free text cannot be promoted to trusted reports. An original observer may be unavailable or dead: a stored snapshot can still be recalled, but existing original-witness reporting/contact rules remain separate.

The history views reuse existing saved relationships, memories, decisions, messages and information. No new save schema or invented legacy history is needed. Actor-specific UI caches reset on world/actor changes. Existing old text-only and browser-import fixtures retain their migration behavior.

## M01 acceptance replay

`--playtest-social-history` verifies a connected social journey:

1. A selfish resident refuses a local meal request. Eighteen real, cooldown-spaced conversations/exchanges create friendship; a later real handover succeeds and its bounded history is browsable.
2. A separate unnoticed theft occurs out of view. The victim's private book stays empty of the thief, including when the inspected actor changes after viewing another person's contacts.
3. A service report passes locally through a real neighbor, then waits in an offline recipient's inbox. That resident walks to a public computer, pays its owner and receives the pending messages. Additional real published notices naturally evict the oldest active report.
4. The recipient recalls that delivered report from the archive with its original dates. A relay dies, the world saves/reloads, and the report, contact IDs, original names, sixteen encounters, eight decisions and twelve-message conversation remain intact. The replay renders older encounter/decision/message pages and checks conserved money and real movement.

Run `private_social_history` for 28 focused rule/privacy/archive/legacy/gate cases. `docs/evidence/m01-completion-verification.json` records installed, memory, localized hardware/software and continuation checks, plus one evidence mapping per M01 criterion.
