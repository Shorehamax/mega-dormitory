# Fire, smoke and damaged shipments

Existing rare seeded accidents, paid fire-station jobs, charged extinguishers, physical travel, attended response and conserved response budgets remain in use. Fire consumes facility stock/condition, burns exposed possessions and harms residents; normal medical transport, body handling and repair rules handle the consequences. Closing a business does not make it fireproof.

## Connected floors and rooms

Strong fires accumulate exposure at combustible businesses with a clear heat path. Same-floor radiant spread reaches eighteen metres. Rising heat can reach up to three levels through continuous actual openings, with reduced strength and a bounded twenty-four-metre path. Intact slabs stop it. The atrium, ladder holes, road/freight cutouts and removed intermediate slabs participate in the same geometry used by support and rendering. A freight platform parked across its landing seals that sampled opening. Wet utilities and transport facilities are not automatic combustible spread targets.

Smoke builds through connected openings one level per update; it does not instantly appear above every fire. The model retains at most eight upper-floor layers per source. Each layer has a saved position and concentration, delayed filling and natural clearance. Horizontal reach and opening size attenuate flow. Full partitions block it; a real doorway gap or space above low furniture can pass gas. Existing upper smoke decays after a route is closed rather than disappearing. A contained source can still have unsafe upper smoke, and the incident is not cleared or its unused response budget refunded until the remaining layers disperse.

Residents sense local heat/smoke through the usual danger and evacuation system. The personal alarm identifies nearby smoke. The fire page lists affected levels; Manager/Cheat can inspect the smoke without relocating or taking control of a resident. Survival does not gain that new remote inspection control. Responders reaching a source already contained by somebody else finish their obsolete travel without restarting paid spray or adding a false interrupted task.

Vulkan ray-marches separate floor-confined volumes. A cached 32 × 32 visibility mask per displayed layer uses the simulation's partition visibility queries; it is refreshed when the origin or building geometry changes. This keeps rendered haze out of most sealed areas and prevents a single source volume being drawn through several intact slabs. The GPU evaluates the masks and ray samples in parallel. Hardware displays at most sixteen nearest layers; software Vulkan displays four. The simulation has its own full bounded layer state and does not depend on the rendering budget. Masks have approximately 1.125-metre horizontal cells and can soften boundaries near thin walls.

This is a gameplay model of connected openings and direct visibility, not fluid dynamics. It does not model airflow around arbitrary corners, pressure, toxicity species, mechanical ventilation, sealed/operable fire doors or a whole-building smoke network. Arbitrary partial floor editing, load-bearing failure and structural collapse remain unfinished.

## Actual property and cargo loss

Direct heat damages real personal items at their actual position, or at their carrier's current position. Smoke alone does not destroy belongings. Ruined possessions retain ownership and identity, lose sale offers and cannot function as working devices or consumable food/medicine. Tools/tableware resist heat more than clothes or food. Extinguisher durability still represents charge and is not treated as casing damage.

Active business imports and waste shipments now have saved lost-unit counts derived from the actual crate's remaining condition. Inbound air cargo uses its animated freight position; carried cargo uses its real carrier. Exposed food, workshop supplies and waste have different resistance. Burned reserved waste is removed from the source pile once; later pickup removes only the surviving reservation.

Partially damaged crates must still be collected, carried and handed over. Only surviving units reach the client's stock or waste depot. Delivery wages and disposal charges are proportional to surviving quantity; the remaining unearned escrow returns to the crate's owner. Full destruction closes the shipping reservation, releases any carrier at the actual location and refunds only unearned logistics/disposal money. Imported goods were already bought from the outside economy and are not refunded or replaced for free. The original title and charred container remain. Work offers show surviving units and the actual payable fee, including cents; the fire page includes damaged-shipment receipts visible to their owners/workers or authorized managers.

Automatic couriers/cleaners avoid burning freight goals, retain carried goods while waiting, and resume physical work after the destination clears. A destination fire stops an automatic walking delivery at its current position; an obsolete suspended arrival evacuates with its cargo instead of handing stock to the fire. Manual residents retain their existing direct actions. Existing automatic restocking can commission another paid shipment after a loss, provided the business can afford it. A courier finishing an old suspended trip cannot deliver stock from a destroyed shipment. Death, save/reload and ownership changes retain the existing physical and accounting rules. Late refunds to an emigrated personal owner flow out to the external economy; a deceased owner’s retained account/estate follows the existing succession rules. Construction-contract material batches keep their previous model and are not made combustible by this pass. Separate ash cleanup, parcel insurance, vehicle fuel fires, ammunition explosions and structural/furniture destruction remain unfinished.

## Saves and verification

Smoke layers, lost-unit counts and the terminal lost-shipment state persist. Both new fields are optional when reading older saves: prior shipments retain all original units and older fires start without invented upper layers. Old incident smoke begins using real opening connectivity on subsequent updates. No new floors, cuts, accidents or cash are injected into existing worlds.

The focused suite covers intact/restored slabs, atrium delay, upper ignition, partition/doorway visibility, freight-platform seals, residual smoke and escrow, render masks, partial/complete/carried freight loss, waste reservations, obsolete responder arrivals, malformed data, round trips and a compressed older-format fixture. The rendered `--playtest-chimney` scenario follows a resident walking away from upper-floor smoke while a lower fire causes delayed upper ignition and destroys a real paid import. These are bounded scripted checks, not exhaustive manual gameplay or long-term balance guarantees.
