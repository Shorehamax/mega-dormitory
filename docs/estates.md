# Personal inheritance and physical belongings

Open **My life → Inheritance & belongings**. Expand **Your inheritance instructions** to nominate another living adult by their displayed resident number. Enter `-1` to remove the nomination. This controls personal property; company shares retain their separate equity-heir controls.

A death opens one estate with a one-game-day review period. The nominee can accept assets and obligations after review or decline. A living household member or a trusted friend may receive an unclaimed offer when the nominated person is unavailable or refuses. Automatic adult heirs can accept a solvent estate. A manually controlled heir, including the player, is never made to accept automatically. Assets without a willing eligible heir remain in the estate.

Acceptance transfers the deceased's actual cash, owned beds, personal businesses, vehicles and possessions. Existing tenants, parked vehicles, customers' stored goods, workplace leases, construction budgets, deliveries and stolen-property claims retain their identity and location. Unfunded property changes require the new owner's consent. Dormitory treasury and dorm ownership are separate governance rights, so a personal inheritance does not grant the building or its common funds.

Death ends the deceased's own bed tenancy. Unpaid rent becomes a retained estate claim instead of disappearing when the bed is made available again. Existing earned wage debts follow an inherited employer; salary owed to the deceased becomes the heir's receivable. Accepting an estate also accepts its recorded obligations. Available estate money pays retained rent claims, with unpaid amounts remaining collectible after acceptance. Storage arrears remain attached to their actual leases. The page shows estimated assets and obligations before acceptance; its accepted debt amount is a historical snapshot, not a current total for every heir-owned business.

Late refunds arriving in the old account are paid onward once. If the accepted heir has emigrated, late money leaves through the external economy. Pending purchases can retain their original escrow and be delivered to the new accepting recipient. Completed transactions remain historical records. Declining an estate does not charge the rejecting resident.

Belongings stay where they were dropped, held, stored or seized. The page lists the heir's unreserved loose goods with walk/collect controls. Nearby owned items can also be collected with **E**. Visible dropped laptops, phones, folded clothes, medicines and small parcels use their actual positions. Private locker contents require reaching and opening the compartment; recovered evidence still requires its custody desk. Inheritance never delivers property remotely or launders a stolen item's provenance.

Automatic heirs can make local trips to collect their inherited loose goods during free time; urgent needs and work take priority. Material crates and waste retain their professional hauling workflows. A dead locker custodian's lease becomes collection-only, preserving contents and debt rather than continuing to spend their wallet. An inherited lease preserves other depositors' ownership and resets shared permissions to private collection.

Wills, review/acceptance status, rent claims, distributed cash and inherited item references save with the world. Earlier saves retain their existing titles and dormant dead accounts; old deaths do not retroactively produce a new inheritance offer. This is a simplified gameplay estate process. Probate jobs, contested wills, automatic asset liquidation, loans/insolvency and full family-law inheritance remain future work.

`personal_estates_and_inheritance` checks nomination, review, consent, physical collection, tenant protection, wages, debt, private storage, stolen-property claims, late payments, emigration, unclaimed assets, saved state and real parcel settlement. `--playtest-estates` starts from an isolated reviewed-estate fixture, accepts a genuinely purchased tenanted bed and cash, then walks to collect the deceased's actual device. It verifies preserved tenancy and continuous movement; the rule suite separately enforces the review delay.
